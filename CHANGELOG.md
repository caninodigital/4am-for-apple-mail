# Release notes

## 1.0.2 — 1 October 2026

- The menu bar panel's header now reads "Shortcuts 4 Apple Mail", spelling out where the name comes from.

## 1.0.1 — 1 October 2026

- Small internal improvements to licensing.

## 1.0.0 — 1 October 2026

First release.

- Gmail-style single-key shortcuts in Apple Mail's message list: archive, delete, next and previous message, reply, reply all, forward, new message, flag, mark as spam, mark read and unread, undo, search and open.
- The same shortcuts in a message opened in its own window.
- `⌘↩` to send from a compose window.
- A menu bar panel showing every shortcut, with switches for turning shortcuts on and opening at login.
- Shortcuts can be remapped or switched off.
- Free 7-day trial, then a one-time license.
