# Release notes

## 1.1.0 — 5 October 2026

- **Shortcut sets.** Choose Gmail, Outlook for Mac, Outlook for Windows, Custom or Off from the menu. Outlook for Windows puts Ctrl shortcuts on the Mac's Control key, including `⌃C`, `⌃V` and `⌃X` to copy, paste and cut.
- **Edit shortcuts in the menu.** Click Edit Shortcuts, click an action and press the key you want. Start from any set and change it from there. No settings file to edit.
- **The menu lists every shortcut**, including the ones that are Apple Mail's own. 4AM's keys are blue, the Mac's are outlined, and three icons show where each works: the message list, an open message, or while writing. A question-mark button explains them.
- **The menu bar icon is bright only while shortcuts are live.** It dims when Mail isn't in front or shortcuts are off.
- Switches are back at the top of the menu and are green when on.
- Shortcuts are paused while the 4AM menu is open.

## 1.0.2 — 1 October 2026

- The menu bar panel's header now reads "Shortcuts 4 Apple Mail", spelling out where the name comes from.

## 1.0.1 — 1 October 2026

- Small internal improvements to licensing.

## 1.0.0 — 1 October 2026

First release.

- Gmail-style single-key shortcuts in Apple Mail's message list: archive, delete, next and previous message, reply, reply all, forward, new message, flag, mark as spam, mark read and unread, undo, search and open.
- The same shortcuts in a message opened in its own window.
- `⌘↩` to send from a compose window.
- A menu bar panel showing every shortcut, with switches for turning shortcuts on and opening at login.
- Shortcuts can be remapped or switched off.
- Free 7-day trial, then a one-time license.
